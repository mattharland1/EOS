Your options

Find the right way to work.

Different assignments call for different arrangements. Explore the options below and understand what they mean for you. The appropriate route depends on the engagement and applicable requirements.

01

Umbrella PAYE

A straightforward employment arrangement with PAYE deductions, employment rights and clear information to help you understand how your pay is calculated.

Explore Umbrella PAYE ↗
02

CIS Subcontractor

For eligible self-employed construction subcontractors, with verification, appropriate deductions and clear payment information under the Construction Industry Scheme.

Explore CIS ↗
03

Sole Trader / Limited Company

For eligible non-CIS business engagements where the contractual relationship and working practices support the arrangement, with appropriate checks and documentation.

Explore registration ↗
04

PAYE Bureau

Where your recruitment agency or engager uses EOS to process its own PAYE payroll, our bureau service supports the payroll administration behind your pay. This is an agency-led arrangement rather than an individual EOS registration route.

Understand PAYE Bureau ↗
International payroll

Working internationally? EOS also supports international payroll arrangements through Safeguard Global, with EOS remaining your point of contact. Availability depends on the country and engagement.

Explore International Payroll ↗
