Our services

Different ways of working.
One clear standard.

From Umbrella PAYE and CIS to PAYE Bureau, non-CIS business engagements and international payroll, EOS brings specialist knowledge and thoughtful processes together.

01

Umbrella PAYE

Umbrella employment for eligible temporary workers, with digital registration, clear pay information and ongoing employment support.

02

CIS Payroll

CIS payment administration for eligible construction subcontractors, with verification, appropriate deductions and supporting records.

Explore CIS Payroll ↗
03

PAYE Bureau

Our PAYE Bureau service supports payroll calculations and reporting for agencies employing their own PAYE workers, while the agency retains its employer responsibilities.

A distinct non-CIS route for eligible business engagements where the contractual relationship and working practices support the arrangement.

International Payroll

Through our partnership with Safeguard Global, agencies can explore international payroll arrangements while retaining EOS as their point of contact. Availability depends on the country and engagement.
