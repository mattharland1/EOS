Five games. 100 points available.

Each fixture has five games, with 20 service points played in each game. Play all five games to record your organisation’s score out of 100.

Singles: two players each serve 10 points per game. Doubles: four players each serve five points per game.

How scoring and rankings work

Each point is awarded to one side. The visiting agency or supplier records its points from each game; EOS receives the remaining points. A game is drawn at 10–10. More than 10 points wins the game.

Count service points rather than individual serve attempts. Any permitted second serve or replayed let remains part of the same point.

Results are ranked separately in the Agency League and Supplier League by total points, then games won, then best game. Identical results share a position.

Each leaderboard entry is one completed fixture. Singles and doubles share their league’s scoring basis, with the format shown. Organisations may appear more than once for separate fixtures.
