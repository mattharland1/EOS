Our story

EOS was created to bring a fresh approach to contracting. A business that combines intelligent technology with real human support, where compliance, fairness and clarity aren't just talked about — they're built into everything we do.

We believe the contracting market should work better for everyone. That means simpler processes, clearer information and a partner you can rely on to help you move forward with confidence.

The principles behind EOS

Four principles. One approach.

Four principles that guide our decisions, shape our approach and define how we work.

01

Clarity

We make the complex straightforward. From onboarding to payday, we believe everyone deserves clear information, simple processes and the confidence of knowing exactly where they stand.

02

Innovation

We challenge outdated ways of working. By combining intelligent technology, purposeful automation and human expertise, we create smarter, more efficient experiences that make contracting easier for everyone.

03

Protection

Compliance isn't an afterthought. It's built into everything we do. Through responsible practices, careful oversight and unwavering integrity, we help protect contractors, recruitment agencies and the wider supply chain.

04

Partnership

We believe progress is built together. By listening, taking ownership and building genuine relationships, we work alongside our contractors and agency partners to create lasting value and shared success.

Built differently

A better way
to work.

Contracting shouldn't be complicated. Yet too often, repeated requests for the same information, disconnected systems and outdated processes make everyday tasks harder and more time-consuming than they need to be.

EOS is being built to change that. From the first registration to every payday, we're rethinking how the experience should work — reducing unnecessary administration, simplifying interactions and making information easier to access and understand.

For contractors, that means fewer obstacles and greater confidence. For recruitment agencies, it means less time spent chasing information and more time focused on their business.

Because the best processes are the ones that make life easier for everyone.
