OUR CHOSEN CHARITY

Supporting
Edward’s Trust.

Edward’s Trust is a Birmingham-based charity providing free, specialist bereavement support to children, young people and parents across the West Midlands.

Through compassionate counselling, wellbeing therapies and peer support, they offer a safe space for people to be heard, find understanding and navigate life after the death of someone close.

HOW THEY HELP

Support when it matters most.

Edward’s Trust provides a range of free services tailored to bereaved children, young people and parents, helping them access the support that feels right for them.

01

Counselling

One-to-one and family counselling with specialist bereavement support professionals.

02

Wellbeing therapies

A range of therapeutic activities that support wellbeing, self-care and emotional resilience.

03

Peer support

Opportunities to connect with others who understand through supportive groups and shared experiences.
