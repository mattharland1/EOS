Contractor Financial

Mortgages and financial protection

Contractor Financial

Contractor Financial helps contractors and self-employed professionals explore mortgages, personal protection and financial planning. Its services recognise umbrella, CIS and limited company working arrangements, with personalised financial reviews and access to pension advice through referral.

  • Contractor mortgage advice
  • Life cover and income protection
  • Financial reviews and pension referrals

CVIA Careers

CVs and career support

CVIA Careers

CVIA Careers helps contractors, interims and freelancers present their experience clearly and strengthen their professional profile. Its services include specialist CV writing, LinkedIn profile development and interview coaching, helping you prepare for your next contract or career move.

  • Specialist contractor CV writing
  • LinkedIn profile development
  • Interview and career coaching

IN-SYNC

Tax and VAT support

IN-SYNC

IN-SYNC supports self-employed workers, including CIS subcontractors, with personal tax returns and VAT administration. Its specialists can manage submissions and provide ongoing support, with tax and VAT refund advances available to eligible customers, subject to fees and terms.

  • Self Assessment and tax support
  • VAT registration and returns
  • Tax and VAT refund advances

Pie Tax

Digital tax management

Pie Tax

Pie Tax helps self-employed people manage income, expenses and Self Assessment through a mobile app. Connect bank accounts, keep digital records and submit returns to HMRC, with Making Tax Digital features and optional paid support from tax specialists when you need extra help.

  • Income and expense tracking
  • Self Assessment and Making Tax Digital
  • Optional expert tax support

Paid referral partnership. EOS receives commission when someone we refer purchases a qualifying paid Pie Tax service.

Radar Insurance

Contractor and business insurance

Radar Insurance

Radar Insurance is a specialist broker for construction and engineering businesses, including contractors and tradespeople. Its team helps arrange cover for business risks, from liability and professional indemnity to tools, plant and contract works, with support at renewal and when claims arise.

  • Contractor insurance quotations
  • Liability and professional indemnity
  • Tools, plant and contract works

Rickna HR

HR and people support

Rickna HR

Rickna HR provides practical HR support for recruitment businesses and growing employers. Its retained and project-based services cover employment contracts, policies, employee relations and ongoing advice, helping you manage your team and put clear working practices in place.

  • Employment contracts and policies
  • Employee relations advice
  • Retained and project HR support

Construction training

Sundial Training provides flexible online health and safety courses for construction workers. Its courses cover asbestos awareness, working at height, manual handling and fire safety, helping CIS subcontractors develop their knowledge and complete training at a time and pace that suits them.
